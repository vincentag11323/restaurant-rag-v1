================================================================================
                    RESORTOS AI - HOSPITALITY ENTERPRISE PLATFORM
                          Portfolio Project Summary for JGS
================================================================================

1. ENTERPRISE CONCEPT & SCALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Domain: High-end multi-venue hospitality platform operating across 2 major branches
  (Branch 1 & Branch 2).
- Operating Hours & Menus: Seasonal Q1–Q4 rotations with fine dining vs. à la carte.
- Core Standard Menu Staples:
  * Chicken Steak
  * Beef Steak
  * Chicken Burger
  * Beef Burger
  * Fish & Chips
  * Barramundi

2. CAPACITY & SEATING LAYOUT (PER BRANCH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Total Simultaneous Capacity: 150 Guests (Main Floor + VIP Wing)
- Main Dining Hall:
  * Capacity: 100 Guests
  * Tables: 50 Tables (2 seats per table)
  * Features: Flexible joining logic for larger party sizes.
- VIP Private Dining Sector:
  * Capacity: 50 Guests
  * Layout: 4 Modular VIP Rooms (~12–13 seats per room)
  * Features: Partition/Joining graph logic allowing 1, 2, 3, or all 4 rooms to merge
    for private corporate/family events.
- Dining Experience Duration Rules: 2.5-hour time blocks.

3. TECH STACK & ENGINEERING ALIGNMENT (JOHN GALT SERVICES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Programming Language: Strictly 100% TypeScript (End-to-end type safety, Zod, Prisma).
- Agent Harness: ECC (`ecc-universal`) for agentic workflows, rule enforcement, 
  and repo-level skills instead of low-code platforms (e.g., n8n).
- Vector Database: ChromaDB (Local persistent vector store with hybrid metadata filtering).
- Dataset: Historical invoice dataset spanning 2020–2025 containing customer contacts,
  party sizes, order histories, seasonal tags (Q1–Q4), and dietary restrictions.

4. CORE SYSTEM MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Module 1: VIP Guest & Seasonal Marketing RAG (Active)
  * Ingests 2020–2025 invoice dataset into ChromaDB with rich metadata tags.
  * Vector search engine paired with hard metadata filtering for campaign drafting.
  * Strict Safety Enforcement: Guarantees that guests with severe seafood or red-meat 
    allergies are automatically excluded from conflicting promotional drops.

- Module 2: Cost Anomaly & Expense RAG Engine (Next Step)
  * Tracks COGS variances, multi-vendor invoice fluctuations, and seasonal 
    ingredient price surges across quarters.

- Module 3: High-Concurrency Seating & CRUD Module (Planned)
  * Handles 1-month advance booking drops, 10-minute pessimistic locking on 
    merged table/room allocations, and AI tool-calling for reservations.
